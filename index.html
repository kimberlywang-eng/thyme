      Settings

      Voice

      Thyme uses the voices already on your device. If it sounds robotic, pick another one, and for the most natural sound install an "enhanced" or "premium" voice in your phone's accessibility settings.

      Optional AI

      Thyme works without AI, using built-in recipes. To look up any dish and ask free-form questions, add your own Anthropic API key. It stays on this device, goes only to Anthropic, and is charged to your own account.
